Share a screen

Let your pet look at a screen, window or tab you choose, to learn words and notice what you're doing.

  • Every 15 seconds it reads the text on it, using Windows' built-in text recognition on this computer. Nothing goes online.
  • Pictures are never saved. Only ordinary lower-case words reach your pet: names, numbers, links, e-mail addresses and codes are skipped.
  • A word sticks after it has seen it three times. Sharing stops when you press Stop or close this window.
