# Changelog

What changed in each VPet release. The newest version is at the top. The release workflow publishes each version's section as its GitHub release notes and shows it in the app's update screen, so write it for players, not developers.

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): while VPet is 0.x, the minor number (0.**2**.0) brings new features and the patch number (0.1.**1**) brings fixes only. A save from any earlier version always loads.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

The first public version.

### Your pet
- Choose one of six eggs (Sky, Walker, Hopper, Aqua, Dash, Shell) and raise the creature that hatches through 12 life stages, from egg to its great form.
- It has needs (food, energy, fun, company, curiosity and health), sleeps at night and grows through its genes and how you care for it.
- Its feelings, thoughts, bond with you and personality come from a small language model that runs on your PC, with no cloud. Its mind starts with instincts and grows up as it learns lessons from what it lives through.
- It learns words from you and talks at its own language level, from baby sounds to full sentences.
- *What is it thinking?* shows how it feels and why, its inner monologue, what it has learned and how its mind is growing.

### On your desktop
- Pet it, carry it, feed it, play with it and talk to it from its right-click menu.
- Every way it might get in your way can be switched off: staying on top of other windows, catching clicks (hold Ctrl to touch it when clicks pass through), coming over to your cursor, moving to a corner while you work, speaking up on its own, and the chime when it needs you.
- It goes quiet while you're busy (fullscreen apps, games, presentations) or in quiet hours, and reminds you to take breaks.

### Privacy
- Everything stays on your PC. The app is offline unless you turn on Internet access. The only download it makes on its own is the mind you choose while installing.
- Learning from what you do (which apps, videos and games) is opt-in. You can see and delete everything it knows.

### Installing
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The installer downloads the pet's mind: *Light* (0.6 GB, runs on almost any PC) or *Full* (2.5 GB, needs 8 GB of memory).
- Updates are opt-in: Status > Privacy > Updates.
